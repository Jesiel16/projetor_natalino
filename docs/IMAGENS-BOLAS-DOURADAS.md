# Imagens das bolas douradas

As três imagens foram geradas com o modo nativo `imagegen` do ChatGPT e são usadas apenas como composições ilustrativas. A landing page identifica essa condição e orienta o visitante a conferir a descrição do item físico no checkout.

## Arquivos finais

- `assets/bolas-douradas-12.webp` — packshot com 12 unidades em grade.
- `assets/bolas-douradas-arvore.webp` — cena editorial de árvore decorada.
- `assets/bolas-douradas-texturas.webp` — detalhe dos acabamentos liso, fosco e glitter.

## Prompts finais

### Packshot das 12 unidades

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: e-commerce product lineup on a genuinely transparent background. Primary request: exactly twelve individual gold Christmas baubles, approximately 6 cm each, displayed separately in a strict 4-column by 3-row arrangement. Subject: 12 spherical ornaments only; four glossy gold, four satin matte gold, four fine glitter gold; each ornament has one small cap and hanging loop. Style/medium: photorealistic catalog product photography. Composition/framing: straight-on view, evenly spaced 4 by 3 grid, no overlap, every ornament fully visible, equal scale, generous outer padding. Lighting/mood: soft controlled studio lighting with consistent shadows. Color palette: champagne gold and warm metallic gold. Materials/textures: glossy, matte and fine glitter plastic ornament finishes clearly distinguishable. Constraints: exactly 12 total ornaments; countable at a glance; no reflections that resemble extra ornaments; no packaging; no tree; no ribbons; no text; no logos; no watermark; no background scene; actual transparent alpha.

### Árvore decorada

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium lifestyle image for a Christmas landing page. Primary request: close editorial photograph of a lush natural-looking Christmas tree decorated with exactly twelve visible gold baubles from one coordinated set. Scene/backdrop: elegant Brazilian living room at night, warm neutral walls, soft background bokeh, no people. Subject: tree branches with 12 countable spherical gold ornaments distributed naturally; mix of glossy, satin matte, and fine glitter finishes; small warm white string lights. Style/medium: photorealistic high-end interior lifestyle photography, natural textures, tasteful rather than overly staged. Composition/framing: vertical-to-square crop, tree fills most of frame, ornaments clearly visible, some negative space around the branch edges. Lighting/mood: warm intimate Christmas light, soft realistic highlights, deep evergreen shadows. Color palette: evergreen, champagne gold, warm ivory, restrained dark red accents. Materials/textures: real pine needles, realistic plastic baubles, velvet ribbon used sparingly. Constraints: exactly 12 visible gold baubles; no people; no gifts blocking the ornaments; no text; no logos; no watermark; no extra colored baubles; no projector product; image is an illustrative lifestyle scene.

### Detalhe dos acabamentos

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: editorial detail image for a Christmas e-commerce landing page. Primary request: close-up still life showing three representative gold Christmas baubles from a coordinated 12-piece set: one glossy, one satin matte, one fine glitter. Scene/backdrop: deep forest-green velvet surface with a small restrained evergreen branch at the edge. Subject: exactly three gold spherical ornaments, equal size, clearly showing the three different finishes. Style/medium: photorealistic luxury editorial product photography. Composition/framing: wide horizontal crop, low three-quarter camera angle, ornaments grouped with breathing room and no overlap. Lighting/mood: soft directional studio light, refined warm highlights, deep green shadows. Color palette: forest green, champagne gold, warm cream. Materials/textures: glossy reflection, smooth matte surface, fine glitter texture, realistic velvet. Constraints: exactly 3 ornaments; no packaging; no text; no logos; no watermark; no people; no extra ornaments.
